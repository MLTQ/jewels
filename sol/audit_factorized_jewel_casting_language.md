# `audit_factorized_jewel_casting_language.py`

## Purpose

Runs registered Gate 0b: whether four compositional physical-role tokens solve the capacity and
rendering failures of Gate 0a's single joint prototype while retaining its independent-fit language
signal and irregular centers.

## Components

### `factorized_residual_metrics`
- **Does**: Measures overall and per-role standardized energy explained and independently audits
  source, serialized, cast, and discrete-decision counts.

### `pairwise_factorized_similarity`
- **Does**: Compares concatenated cell-conditional role histograms for equivalent and unrelated
  fields, with per-role margins retained for diagnosis.

### `_save_codebook`
- **Does**: Saves all role prototypes and the shared normalization/address contract.

### `main`
- **Does**: Reuses Gate 0a's exact fields and render controls, fits increasing per-role vocabulary
  sizes, audits residual arms, compares against the matched joint-token baseline, and evaluates the
  frozen Gate-0b checks.

## Contracts

| Dependent | Expects | Breaking changes |
|---|---|---|
| Gate 0b protocol | Same split, validation fits, random points, and joint K1024 baseline | Experimental ownership |
| Evidence plotter | Schema `factorized-jewel-casting-language-gate-v1` | Report schema |
| Future caster | Saved four-role codebook and exact count/anchor program | Codebook layout |
