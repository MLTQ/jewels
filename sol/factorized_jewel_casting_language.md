# `factorized_jewel_casting_language.py`

## Purpose

Defines a compositional Jewel-casting phrase after Gate 0a falsified one joint 176-dimensional
prototype. Layout, covariance, surface, and color-gradient roles receive independent vocabularies;
their aligned prototypes compose back into the same irregular eight-Jewel constellation.

## Components

### `FACTOR_DIMENSIONS`
- **Does**: Freezes the canonical 22-feature partition into four exhaustive, non-overlapping
  physical roles.

### `FactorCodebook` / `FactorizedCodebook` / `FactorizedProgram`
- **Does**: Owns role prototypes, shared normalization/address contracts, role token sequences, and
  exact residual targets.

### `fit_factorized_codebook`
- **Does**: Samples the same source-disjoint canonical casts for every role and fits deterministic,
  count-aware Lloyd vocabularies.
- **Rationale**: Four 1,024-way decisions provide combinatorial capacity without requiring a
  `1024^4` joint lookup table.

### `encode_factorized_program` / `composed_prototypes` / `decode_factorized_program`
- **Does**: Assigns one token per physical role, composes aligned Jewel rows, and decodes any finite
  residual scale. Full residual must round-trip numerically.

### `factor_histograms`
- **Does**: Builds cell-addressed role-token histograms for independent-fit canonicality tests.

### `load_factorized_codebook`
- **Does**: Restores the portable factor/codebook artifacts emitted by audits for later rendering
  and generator training.

## Contracts

| Dependent | Expects | Breaking changes |
|---|---|---|

| Evidence renderer | Saved factor order, dimensions, prototypes, and normalization round-trip | Artifact schema |
